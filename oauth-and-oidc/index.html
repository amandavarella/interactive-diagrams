🎒 Who holds what (after this step)

🔑 access_token
Answers "what can you touch?". Slack sends it to the Calendar API. Slack shouldn't use it as proof of who you are.
🪪 id_token (OIDC only)
Answers "who are you?". A signed JWT that stays with Slack. Slack shouldn't send it to APIs.
🙈 Your password
Typed only on Google's page. Slack never sees it at any step. Watch the holder cards to confirm.
Endpoint URLs and scope names reflect Google's documented values as of mid-2026 and are not live-checked. Codes, tokens, and IDs are fake.
